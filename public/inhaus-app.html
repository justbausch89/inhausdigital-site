What’s inside

One login. Everything that matters.

True attribution

A first-party pixel stamps every visit and click onto every order, and shows what each channel really drove next to what the ad platforms claim.

AI account brains

A dedicated AI analyst for each channel, with a living memory of your account: its rules, its history and every change. It watches daily and reports weekly.

Live business P&L

Revenue minus product costs, ad spend and your real operating expenses, every day. Scale what’s profitable, not what merely has a pretty ROAS.

Daily watchdog

Silent when things are healthy, loud the moment a campaign bleeds, a feed breaks or spend drifts. Weekly deep-dives land automatically.

Your own workspace

Your dashboards, your P&L and your AI brain in a workspace of your own, with your own login to see everything.

Wired into your stack

Shopify, Meta, Google, Klaviyo, Pinterest and Slack, with server-side conversions sent back so the ad platforms learn from real sales.

Custom bolt-ons

Tools built for your business, not everyone’s.

Off-the-shelf software makes you fit its shape. We add bespoke modules to your workspace when your growth needs them.

UGC creative generator

On-brand image and video creatives generated from your products, for fresh ad angles without the shoot.

Lead finder

A prospector that finds, qualifies and exports the businesses you should be talking to.

Product creator

Generate product imagery, variants and listings, and publish straight to Shopify in minutes.

AI support copilot

Reads your support inbox, checks real order status and drafts accurate replies in your brand voice.

Fulfilment & ops suite

Production dashboards, courier scorecards, automated returns and staff output reports.
