Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

InHaus Digital (“we”, “us”) is an Australian marketing business. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us. When you send an enquiry or apply for a workshop, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, email address, phone number, business name or website, your answers and your message.
  • How you found us. If you arrive from an ad or a link, we record which campaign it was and the page you landed on, and attach that to your enquiry.
  • How the site is used. Our website host, Cloudflare, gives us anonymous visitor statistics such as pages viewed, the referring site and the type of device. This doesn’t use cookies or identify you.
  • Advertising measurement. We may use advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel to understand how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform. When active, these tools use cookies and share information about your visit, such as the pages you viewed and whether you sent us a form, with the advertising platform. You can manage how Meta uses this in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or application and arrange a time to talk.
  • To plan and deliver the services or workshops you ask about.
  • To understand which of our pages and ads are working, and improve them.
  • To send you updates or marketing only where you’ve asked for them or agreed to them. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website and our business, such as our website host, our form and email providers and our advertising platforms, and only so they can provide those services to us. Some of these providers store information outside Australia.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. To do that, or to raise a privacy concern, contact us and we’ll respond within a reasonable time. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
