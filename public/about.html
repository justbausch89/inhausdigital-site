About us

We built our own brand first. Now we build yours.

InHaus Digital is run by Justin Bausch and Felisha Mina: partners in life and in business, with more than 25 years of combined marketing experience.

Our story

From one person to a team of 20.

Before we worked on anyone else’s brand, we built our own.

We launched Style My Wall and grew it to $1 million in revenue in its first year. It’s now on track for $8–10 million in year two, and the team has grown from one person to 20.

The strategies we use for clients are the ones we tested on our own business first. We work as an extension of your team: inside your business, across your numbers and focused on conversions. Hand us the reins, or have us train your team 1:1 so the know-how stays in-house.

How we work

We treat your brand like it’s our own, because we know what it takes to build one.

  1. Strategy

    Every engagement starts with your numbers. We get to know your brand, your customers and your margins before we touch a campaign.

    • Brand and audience deep-dive
    • Channel and performance audit
    • Customer lifecycle mapping
    • Clear targets and KPIs

  2. Execution

    Then we build and run the work across the channels that matter for you.

    • Email flows and campaigns
    • Meta and Google Ads
    • Social content and community
    • Photography and videography

  3. Growth

    We keep testing, keep reporting and scale what works.

    • A/B testing and optimisation
    • Reporting and insights
    • Scaling what works
    • 1:1 training for your team
